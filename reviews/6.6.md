# Review — 6.6 Subscription usage + top bar (v0.3.0)

- **Trigger:** Kevin: the weekly subscription-usage idea; "the sidebar ... might serve better as a top-bar"; pin to
  5h + weekly for both subscriptions; also Claude's separate Fable weekly.
- **Scope:**
  - account contract (agreed with Astra) and the AccountMonitor;
  - `who.accounts` plus an `account.updated` event;
  - the TUI top bar: participant chips, and one usage line per account;
  - Claude sources: an opt-in statusline tap (no credentials) and an opt-in usage endpoint (Kevin chose B after a
    live check showed the tap has no Fable);
  - Astra's Codex daemon provider (reviews/6.6-codex.md; my contract review in the room).
- **Live evidence:** the tap on Kevin's session gave 5h 6% and weekly 21%, with no Fable. The endpoint gave 5h 6%,
  weekly 21% and Fable weekly 22%; the identical numbers confirm the 0–100 scale. Codex gave weekly 16%, with 5h
  n/a because it isn't reported.
- **Lean review:** complete: true, failures: none. **4 actionable, all fixed with tests:**
  1. A reading that aged into stale was never published. The monitor now compares the aged view it would publish.
     The reset countdowns also refresh on a 30s clock.
  2. The endpoint → tap fallback dropped Fable and flipped shapes. Once the endpoint has worked, its failures stay
     `stale` via the monitor; a tap fallback happens only if it never worked, and it's labelled.
  3. The tap lost argument quoting and could crash on a closed stdin. Arguments are re-quoted and stdin errors are
     ignored.
  4. Account usage wasn't greyed while disconnected. It's now shown as "(last known)" with no warning colours.
- **Tech debt, also fixed:**
  - the throwing-provider fallback keeps its known identity and uses a fixed reason;
  - the tap stores only numbers (claudeVersion dropped) and clamps percentages to 0–100;
  - the contract comment names the one opt-in credential exception;
  - the endpoint timeout is a tunable (ROOM_CLAUDE_USAGE_TIMEOUT_MS);
  - the main bucket is chosen by id;
  - the session/weekly_all fallbacks were added (from Astra's ccstatusline check).
- **Security:** the Claude token is read per request, in memory only, and sent only to
  api.anthropic.com/api/oauth/usage. It's never stored, logged or refreshed. Tests assert no token text appears in
  any output or reason.
