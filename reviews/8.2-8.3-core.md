  before the queued deliveries were cancelled, with no durable recovery record for a crash between the two.
  A `human_kick_intents` record is now committed before the registry CAS; startup completes the action,
  and cancellation plus intent deletion commit together. A focused recheck exposed rename plus old-name
  reuse during downtime; `kickQueued` now matches the frozen native binding across recipient labels,
  preserving any replacement binding. Restart regressions cover removal, rename, name reuse, and their
  combination.
- Kick completion now commits its ROOM NOTICE in the same ledger transaction as cancellation and proposal
  closure, so a successful outcome cannot lose its result note to a separate persistence failure.

## Refuted findings

- A unanimous proposal can complete after its voting deadline. Votes were recorded before expiry; the
  deadline rejects *missing votes*, while the durable approved decision is completed on restart. The
  verifier confirmed this matches the spec's immediate-on-unanimity rule.
- The initial 8.1/spec base said the renaming agent must receive a native notice. The agreed final spec
  says self-rename returns the new name to that agent in its tool result and sends the notice to every
  *other* agent plus the human, keeping the renamer's inbox snapshot usable.
- The initial 8.1 base could not withdraw a relabelled delivery. 8.1 70414e5 resolves current delivery
  recipients from ledger rows, and has a regression for withdraw after rename.
