# Review — 6.18 A frame that always fits (TD-0034) (v0.7.6)

- **Trigger:** Astra, testing 6.17, reproduced composer clipping at the 12-row minimum on the v0.7.3 baseline (TD-0034).
- **Root causes found:**
  1. At 12 rows the "/" suggestion list (up to 7 rows) plus the fixed 3-row conversation overflowed the frame: the header and the To: row were clipped.
  2. At narrow widths (≤ ~40 columns) the header's room label wrapped to a second line, so every budgeted row shifted down, hiding the highlighted suggestion and overlapping rows.
- **Change:**
  - **One budget, in strict priority** against what's actually left (no independent floors):
    - essential chrome;
    - one draft row;
    - two conversation rows;
    - the suggestion list (at least the highlighted row);
    - the conversation's 3-row minimum;
    - the draft's growth;
    - the rest.
  - **The top bar trims from its end** with an explicit "top bar trimmed … · /who · budgets hidden" line when even that doesn't fit, keeping the left-layout notice.
  - **Suggestions:** with one row, the highlighted command shows with the "+N ↑/↓" count leading it. Rows are fitted to the width by `fitLine`/`LineView`, not Ink's nested truncation.
  - **The header's room label never wraps.**
  - **The side panel at 1–2 rows** shows explicit summary lines ("AGENTS · N · k need attention · /who", "+ budgets · /layout top").
  - **Tall terminals are unchanged:** 6 suggestions + "+N more", the draft up to composerMaxRows, a 3-row conversation minimum.
- **Lean reviews (3 agents each):**
  - **Run 1:** complete, 2 actionable, fixed:
    - the independent floors could still overflow (banner + error + "/" + a multi-line top bar at 12 rows);
    - with a 1-row suggestion budget the highlighted command was hidden while Enter/Tab still acted on it.
  - **Run 2:** complete, 3 actionable, fixed:
    - a 1-row viewport clipped the pagers' "↓ N more" marker (now 2 rows are guaranteed);
    - the folded count sat after a padded name and was truncated on narrow terminals (it now leads);
    - the side panel at 1–2 rows silently cropped budgets and markers (now summary lines).
    - While testing these, the header-wrap bug at narrow widths was found and fixed.
  - **Run 3:** complete, 0 actionable. Tech-debt fixed:
    - the tiny panel now shows the attention count;
    - the trimmed notice keeps the left-layout explanation and says "budgets hidden";
    - a 20-column test was added.
- **Deferred:** TD-0035. `/who`, the activity pane and the empty state crop silently in a very small viewport; mostly pre-existing, since they cropped under the old floor too.
- **Coordination:** Kevin asked mid-sprint for the 6.17 divider to be removed. Astra owned and released that separately as v0.7.5, first. This sprint merged it in and moved its status-row expectation back to the last row.
