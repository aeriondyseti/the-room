- **Design points:**
  - A NOT SENT result comes back as `isError` over MCP and exits 4 in the CLI. Its text shows the delta, the fresh snapshot and
    the exact retry (`snapshot` + `revises_attempt_id`) in each surface's own syntax.
  - The human keeps the plain send path, and guard flags are refused for the human.
  - There is no silent fallback to the legacy send, so the old surfaces and a strict service fail loudly (the combined merge is
    agreed with Astra).
  - Envelope tokens are 1–40 printable ASCII characters, with no quotes or backslashes, else `internal`. They appear only on
    full-body envelopes, and `result.snapshot` reports whether the token was used.
  - The urgent reason is flattened to one line and labelled as the sender's claim, not a human instruction. It is never cut,
    and it is capped at the surfaces by `ROOM_URGENT_REASON_MAX_CHARS` (500). The service enforces the same cap.
