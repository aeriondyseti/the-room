## Tech debt worked immediately
TUI quality is the priority and these bite with a real room:
- **Reconnect during an in-flight load** was dropped. It now re-runs the load for the new connection.
- **Stale snapshot rollback.** Events that arrive during a load are buffered and re-applied on top of the
  snapshot.
- **Terminal escapes and control characters** in bodies and names could spoof headers (e.g. a fake
  "Kevin →" line). A new `sanitize()` makes C0/C1, ESC, DEL and bidirectional overrides visible and
  inert.
- **Wide characters.** Wrapping and fitting now measure terminal columns (`string-width`) and split by
  grapheme, so emoji and CJK text can't overflow and get silently cut.
- **Header fitting by priority.**
  - The seq and reply ref are dropped before recipients.
  - The sender and `(declared)` are never dropped.
  - Delivery marks wrap onto extra lines instead of hiding a state behind `…`.
- **Failed load** no longer sticks on "Loading room…": it shows an explicit error with `r` to retry.
- **While disconnected**, the participant panel is marked `· stale` and shows "last known" status.
